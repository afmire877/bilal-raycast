# Bilal

Unofficial Raycast companion for [Bilal](https://bilalathan.co.uk), which tells you when to leave for jama'ah.

## Setup

1. Open [bilalathan.co.uk/calendar](https://bilalathan.co.uk/calendar), pick your mosque and prayers.
2. Choose "Copy the calendar link".
3. Paste it into the extension's **Calendar Link** preference.

Only prayers included in your calendar link are shown. Missing Isha? Tick it on the calendar page and copy the link again.

## Preferences

- **Calendar Link**: your mosque's calendar from bilalathan.co.uk/calendar
- **Time Away**: minutes for wudu and walking there, counted back from the jama'ah
- **Menu Bar Title**: prayer and countdown, or countdown only

## Commands

- **Next Jama'ah**: upcoming jama'ah by day, with when to leave. Filter by prayer, ⌘D for details
- **Jama'ah Countdown**: menu bar countdown to when you should leave

Bilal Near and Bilal Display open from either.

Works offline from the last saved timetable. Independent jama'ah-time service: please confirm any changes with the mosque.
